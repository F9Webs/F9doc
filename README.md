# F9Doc HMIS – Hospital Management Information System

F9Doc HMIS is a comprehensive, web-based Hospital Management Information System designed to streamline hospital operations, digitize healthcare workflows, and simplify patient management. Built using modern web technologies, it provides an integrated platform for managing hospital departments, clinical activities, administrative operations, and financial processes.

The system aims to improve operational efficiency, enhance patient care coordination, and provide healthcare professionals with a centralized solution for managing day-to-day hospital activities.

**Key Modules & Features:**
- Patient Registration & Medical Records
- Appointment & OPD Management
- IPD & Admission Management
- Doctor, Staff & Department Management
- Electronic Medical Records (EMR)
- Clinical Notes, Prescriptions & Investigations
- Billing, Accounts & Financial Management
- Pharmacy & Inventory Management
- Doctor Timetables & Scheduling
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Dashboard, Reports & Analytics
- ABDM Integration Support

**Technology Stack:**
- Backend: PHP 8.2 / CodeIgniter 4
- Database: MySQL
- Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap 5
- Architecture: Modular, AJAX-powered web application

**Project Vision**

F9Doc HMIS aims to deliver a scalable, secure, and efficient digital healthcare management solution that helps hospitals and healthcare institutions transition towards organized, technology-driven operations.
